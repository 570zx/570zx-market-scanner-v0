What this page does not do
  • It cannot download from YouTube. Use the original file of your video.
  • No automatic captions and no speaker tracking. Pick the crop position yourself.
  • Quality is set by your phone's video recorder, not the studio-grade encode of the computer clipper.
  • iPhone saves MP4. Some Android browsers save WebM, which TikTok and Instagram may refuse.
