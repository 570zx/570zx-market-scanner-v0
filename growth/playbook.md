# Playbook

Running record. Every tactic gets a verdict once it has data: **keep**, **drop**, or **retest**.

## What works (baseline from existing posts, Aug–Oct 2026)
- **One-line concept titles** (Hemi + Z32 + 1:1 widebody). Ep 1 is at 6k views, 2× any other video.
- **Show partner news, don't announce it.** The "getting kinda serious" tease got 2,664 TikTok views; the formal announcement got 242.
- **Standalone clip with the link in the caption.** "Dirty yet so clean" did 1.4k on TikTok and 2.3k on IG.
- **Engagement prompts** (the A/B/C poll): 90–100 likes and lots of comments.
- **Headlight content.** 2.2k IG views in about a day.

## What flops
- Pure "video is live" / "site is live" posts (20–186 views)
- Formal long-caption partner announcements
- Posting gaps: a 19-day gap dropped YouTube to 2–17 views a day
