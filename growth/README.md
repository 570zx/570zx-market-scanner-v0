# 570ZX organic growth

Working files for getting Dodge's videos in front of more people without paid ads.

**The rule:** Dodge makes all the content. This folder only covers distributing his real work. No fake comments, sock puppets, bots, or bought engagement. Anything written in his voice, and anything public, needs his approval first.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `reports/` | Dated research reports and weekly reports |
| `communities.md` | Where the audience hangs out, each place's self-promo rules, and whether the rules are verified |
| `opportunities.md` | Creators, press/newsletters, search gaps, local events |
| `queue.md` | Items ready to go, each with an approval status |
| `playbook.md` | Running record of what worked, what flopped, and what to try next |

## Loop

1. **Discover:** analytics, communities, trends, creators, search gaps, submission targets.
2. **Prepare:** clips from his footage, drafts, pitches. All of it goes into `queue.md`.
3. **Execute:** research, clipping, analytics, and playbook updates run without asking. Posting, outreach, and anything public waits for the daily approval batch, which he answers after 5 PM ET.
4. **Learn:** track views, watch time, subs, and email signups per tactic, then update `playbook.md`.
