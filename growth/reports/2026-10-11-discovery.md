### Patterns
1. **The headline concept carries the long-form.** Episode 1's title packs Hemi + Z32 + 1:1 widebody into one line and has twice the views of anything else.
2. **Hype beats formal announcements.** The "getting kinda serious" tease got about 11× the views of the formal 3DXTECH announcement on the same platform two days later. Partner news should be shown, not announced.
3. **Link-drop posts flop.** Every "video is live / site is live" post landed between 20 and 186 views. A clip that stands on its own, with the link in the caption ("Dirty yet so clean"), does 10× better.
4. **The same clip can win on one platform and die on another** ("No Discharge": 2,289 on IG, 56 on YT). Keep posting every clip to all three, and note that the winner changes from clip to clip.
5. **Headlights are a new growth topic.** The Oct 10 headlight reel hit 2.2k on IG in about a day, faster than any earlier reel. The headlight audience (lighting and retrofit people, Morimoto fans) is wider than Z32 owners.
6. **Consistency matters.** The 19-day gap with no posts flattened every platform.
