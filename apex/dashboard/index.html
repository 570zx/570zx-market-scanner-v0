Strategy status

Every strategy family tested, best first. A verdict is set by rule: a strategy must make money after costs on data it was never tuned on, and beat simply holding its market for the risk taken. Round-two rows were tested on 2023 to 2026 against the better of holding SPY and holding QQQ. Older-sample rows re-run the two leading rules on years before 2016 that no other test touched. Rows from MEDS are re-scored from its stored backtests, not re-run.

What the terms mean

Sharpe: return divided by how much the value swings, per year, measured on data the strategy was not tuned on. Holding the S&P 500 scored — from 2019 to 2026. Higher is better, and it is the fairest way to compare a strategy that is only sometimes invested with one that always is.

Largest drop: the biggest fall from a high to a later low. Proven: beat holding on unseen data and passed both statistical tests. Provisional: ahead of holding for the risk taken, not statistically proven. No edge: only a smaller drop than holding. Fragile: works at one setting and not its neighbours. Inconclusive: too few trades to say. Failed: lost money after costs or was no better than holding.

Settings tried: how many versions of the idea were tested. The more that were tried, the better the best one looks by luck alone.
